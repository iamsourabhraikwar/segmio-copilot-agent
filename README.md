---

## 🌟 Overview

Segmio Copilot bridges the gap between natural language prompts and programmatic video production. Instead of manually rotoscoping video layers or adjusting keyframes in desktop editors, creators can direct a video production copilot that understands scene semantics, extracts subjects, and compiles dynamic Remotion timelines in real time.

### Core Capabilities:
* **Zero-Shot Video Matting & Rotoscoping**: Utilizes **Meta SAM 3.1** to segment and track dynamic subjects across video frames without green screens.
* **Agentic Timeline Planning**: Powered by **Meta Muse Spark Open** to parse multi-step creative directives into structured timeline manifests and Remotion compositions.
* **Low-Latency Conversational Co-pilot**: Uses **Fireworks AI** for ultra-fast time-to-first-token (TTFT) structured tool calling and streaming editor updates.
* **Serverless GPU Processing**: Runs computer vision pipelines and batch rendering inside **Modal Sandboxes & GPU Endpoints** and **Baseten** high-throughput Truss clusters.
